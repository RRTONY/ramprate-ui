Joint decisions

The questions we should answer together

Which decisions matter most now?

Identify the two or three strategic choices where experienced outside judgment would make the greatest difference, without assuming a particular project must come first.

Who should be at the table?

Review the advisors and relationships Guy already has, then identify the missing experience, credibility and access across the four domains.

What should each advisor actually do?

Agree where periodic challenge, hands-on decision support, strategic introductions or sustained involvement would be most useful, and how each contribution will be measured.

How should the council work?

Define a practical cadence, decision rights, confidentiality expectations, engagement terms and compensation, with flexibility to start small and expand deliberately.

Where does RampRate's role begin and end?

Clarify who recruits and coordinates advisors, who makes decisions, who owns execution, and what a bounded first phase should deliver. Guy retains direction and operating authority.

A practical way forward

Begin with a conversation about the decisions ahead.

A working session with Guy and the RampRate team would help establish where outside experience could be most valuable, who might bring it, and whether there is a useful role for RampRate in bringing those people together.
