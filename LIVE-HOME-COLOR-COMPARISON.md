# Live Home Color Comparison

**Reference:** [https://ramprate.com/](https://ramprate.com/)

The live home page uses a photo-led hero in which the building and sunset remain the dominant visual. Its readable left-side copy surface is supplied by a restrained near-black to transparent vignette, while the right side preserves the warm amber light in the photograph. The live page pairs that hero with deep charcoal-plum content bands, thin warm-gold rules, white editorial headings, muted lilac body copy, and small amber proof accents.

The feature-branch home page retains the same editorial content structure but currently applies stronger violet and amber washes in several surrounding components. The requested alignment will therefore keep the established cards, links, content, and responsive layout while reducing opaque color veils, tightening surfaces to the live dark-plum palette, and reserving gold primarily for actions, proof markers, and headline emphasis.

This comparison is limited to the home route. No non-home page, CMS, Flow, content, or data behavior is in scope for the live-style alignment.

## Local Review After Alignment

Desktop and 390px mobile full-page checks confirm that the home hero still keeps the office image as the dominant visual while the left-side copy remains readable. All non-hero sections now use one continuous espresso-to-brown RampRate palette with warm-gold actions and proof markers, rather than the earlier violet-heavy treatment. Cards, timeline, testimonials, practice links, client wall, newsletter, compensation, final CTA, and footer remain legible at both sizes.
