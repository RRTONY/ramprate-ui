AI puts you and your creators in your zone of genius.

Technology should free people to do what they do best: creators to create and grow, and StarNews to build relationships and lasting commercial value.

Make creativity easier to start, finish and share.

07 / A new commercial layer

From an audience to market intelligence.

Two early enterprise deals show the demand. A repeatable business still has to be built.

$2K⁠–⁠$6KValues of two early enterprise audience-query deals; StarNews-reported examples
$500K+RampRate’s ambition for larger enterprise engagements; a commercial target, not a forecast or committed pipeline

The concept

Data farming: the people who grow the data share in the harvest.

How it works

Subscribers and creators opt in, own what they generate and get paid when it is licensed

Enterprises buy permissioned insight into African consumers, priced to its value

Bluenumber, a RampRate client, has already run this model with farmers and factory workers

What we need to validate

Before scaling, we'll test how consent and data ownership would work, whether enterprise buyers will pay for the insights, and how licensing revenue could be shared fairly with the people who generate the data.

  • Define what StarNews can responsibly measure, what buyers will pay for and what delivery needs
  • Assess data, permissions, buyer needs and delivery requirements first
  • Draft an initial proposition and list the gaps to close before any market testing or major introductions

12 / Advisor economics

Pay for commitment.
Vest for contribution.

Advisors have to deliver real results to vest most of their allocation. A title alone earns very little.

01 / Define

Agree the mandate.

  • A written remit for each advisor
  • The decisions to shape, doors to open and work to advance

02 / Commit

Match equity to the role.

  • Core strategic advisors: about four hours a month, with rollover
  • Access-led advisors: a lighter commitment
  • Active builders: separately scoped mandates

03 / Earn

Vest against delivery.

  • Strategic advisors: sustained participation, qualified introductions, access to decision-makers
  • Hands-on builders: defined deliverables, commercial progress, measurable operating results
  • Time-based participation where it fits
