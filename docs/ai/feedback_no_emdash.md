# No Emdash

> Chitraket does not want em dashes or long-dash connectors used in EOD/SOD reports or messages

Do not use em dashes (—) or similar long-dash "connector" punctuation in EOD updates, SOD plans, or other messages written for Chitraket. Use periods, commas, "with", "since", "so", or colons instead to join clauses.

**Why:** Chitraket explicitly asked to stop using long dashes in reports/messages, flagged as a standing formatting preference, not a one-off correction. Confirmed a second time on 2026-07-13 after it recurred in an EOD, this time across multiple task bullets in the same message, so it needs active checking on every draft, not just a one-time fix.

**How to apply:** When writing feedback-eod-format or feedback-sod-format reports, or any Slack/email-style message on Chitraket's behalf, proofread the FULL drafted text for the — character specifically before sending, not just while composing. Rephrase any sentence that would normally use " — " into two sentences or a comma/colon join instead. This applies to every single message, including quick edits/follow-ups to a message already sent in the conversation.
